Living-composer share

At a glance

What the data show
1842–43 29.2% → 2025–26 15.8% (completed seasons). The peak was 1844–45 (54.5%; base: 22 work performances with known dates). For 2025–26 the range is 11.3%–39.7%, depending on how composers with unknown dates are counted.
Watch for
Date coverage falls to 72–81% in the 2020s and most composers without dates are presumably alive, so recent values are likely too low — read them with the upper bound. 'Alive in the start year' is a year-level approximation.

Share of work performances by composers alive at the time (new in 1.1).

Formula
work performances by composers alive in the season's start year / work performances by composers with known dates. Dates from Wikidata (529 composers with 10+ performances, reviewed). Lower and upper bounds treat composers without dates as all dead / all living.
How to read it
Decade medians: 41% in the 1840s, 22% in the 1900s, 7–11% from the 1970s to the 2000s, 14% in the 2020s (all completed seasons). The minimum is 3.7% in 1993–94. 2025–26: 15.8% (bounds 11.3%–39.7%).
Caveats
Recent seasons have many emerging composers with fewer than 10 performances, so date coverage falls to 72–81% in the 2020s. Most of them are presumably alive, so recent values are likely too low — read them with the upper bound. 'Alive in the start year' is a year-level approximation.

New York Philharmonic Performance History: works, composers and conductors on more than 15,000 programs from the first concert in December 1842 to the current season. Besides the Philharmonic proper, the archive holds programs of the New York Symphony, the Stadium concerts and chamber series; this site does not filter them out. Updated nightly. https://github.com/nyphilarchive/PerformanceHistory · License: CC0 1.0

Wikidata (Wikimedia Foundation) person items: year of birth (P569) and death (P570). Composers with at least 10 NY Phil performances (U1) were searched by name; only human items with a music occupation were kept and checked against the composer's first season (born at least five years earlier). Names with several candidates or a failed check were resolved one by one by the editor. The matching table (composer_wikidata.csv) is published. https://www.wikidata.org/ · License: CC0 1.0
