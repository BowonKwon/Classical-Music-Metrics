Diminished-seventh ratio

At a glance

What the data show
Among the collections, Beethoven's piano sonatas have the highest median (0.044) and Bach's English and French Suites the lowest (0.019). Within the piano sonatas: Beethoven 0.044, Mozart 0.027.
Watch for
Only for annotated corpora (not chorales). Leading-tone and applied diminished sevenths are not separated.

Share of chord labels that are diminished sevenths.

Formula
chord labels with chord_type o7 / all chord labels
How to read it
How often this tension chord appears. Beethoven sonatas (0.044) and quartets (0.041) are more than double Bach's suites (0.019); Mozart 0.027.
Caveats
Only for annotated corpora (not chorales). Leading-tone and applied diminished sevenths are not separated. Beethoven's piano sonatas enter with the 64 of 91 movements that have harmony annotation.
