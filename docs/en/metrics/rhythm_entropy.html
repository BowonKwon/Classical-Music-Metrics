Rhythm entropy

How evenly different note durations are mixed.

Formula
H = −Σ pᵢ log₂ pᵢ over duration values (in quarter notes, after tying).
How to read it
One duration only gives 0; two in equal share give 1. Below 1.0 is nearly uniform motion; near 2 means four or more durations in balance.
Caveats
A relative measure independent of tempo and meter.
