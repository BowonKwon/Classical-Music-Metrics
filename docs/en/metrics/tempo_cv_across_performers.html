Performance metricsUnit: CV

Across-performer tempo spread

How much performers of the same piece differ in average tempo.

Formula
SD / mean of each performance's mean tempo (60 / mean inter-beat interval)
How to read it
0.05 means performers cluster within ±5% of the mean tempo; above 0.1 interpretations diverge markedly.
Caveats
Performers are International Piano-e-Competition entrants (MAESTRO), not commercial recording artists. Performances per piece range from 2 to 25.
