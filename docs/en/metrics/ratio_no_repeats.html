Performed / metronome duration

At a glance

What the data show
With the theoretical time computed without repeats, movement medians run 0.96–2.00× (lowest: Symphony 6/4; highest: 6/3).
Watch for
A whole-movement average, so tempo changes, rubato and fermatas are folded in. Repeat choices vary by recording and are not in the data, so 2× does not mean half the tempo.

Track length divided by the theoretical length if played at Beethoven's marks without repeats.

Formula
track seconds / Σ sections [(bars − first-ending bars) × beats per bar / mark × 60]. A lower bound assuming all repeats is also given (ratio_with_repeats).
How to read it
1.00 means as marked; 1.20 means the performance is 20% longer (a tempo of 1/1.2, about 17% slower, or repeats taken). Since repeats are unknown, the truth lies between the two ratios. Symphony 9 is included in this table.
