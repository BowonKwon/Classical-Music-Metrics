Bach share

At a glance

What the data show
1842–43 0.0% → 2025–26 1.0% (completed seasons). The peak was 1901–02 (7.7%; 10 concerts, 78 work performances).
Watch for
Counts entries credited to 'Bach, Johann Sebastian' only (joint credits such as 'Vivaldi-Bach' are not), arrangements included. One archive's record, which mixes in programs of related bodies (New York Symphony, Stadium concerts).

Share of a season's work performances that are J. S. Bach.

Formula
Bach work performances / all work performances
How to read it
Season median 1.7%. Zero in 32 of 184 completed seasons; the peak is 7.7% in 1901–02. The 1.0 peak, 2019–20 (9.8%), is 4.8% in the new unit — many entries listed several pieces of one work separately.
Caveats
Arrangements (e.g. Stokowski) count as Bach. Only entries whose composer is 'Bach, Johann Sebastian' are counted; joint credits such as 'Vivaldi-Bach' are not.
