Canon turnover

At a glance

What the data show
Median overlap of the top 10 with the previous decade: 0.54. Most change in the 1850s (0.33), least in the 1930s and 2000s (0.82).
Watch for
The 1840s have 8 seasons and the 2020s 7 including the one in progress (the 1840s have no earlier decade to compare). Ties at the cut-off are broken alphabetically, so one name changing at the boundary moves the index a lot.

How much each decade's list of most-performed composers overlaps with the previous decade's (new in 1.1).

Formula
Jaccard index |A ∩ B| / |A ∪ B| for the top 10 (or 25) composers of this and the previous decade. 1 = same list, 0 = no overlap.
How to read it
Top 10: median 0.54 (roughly seven of ten stay), range 0.33 (1850s) to 0.82 (1930s, 2000s). Top 25: highest in the 2020s at 0.85.
Caveats
The 1840s have 8 seasons and the 2020s 7 including the one in progress. Ties at the cut-off are broken alphabetically, so one name changing at the boundary moves the index a lot.
