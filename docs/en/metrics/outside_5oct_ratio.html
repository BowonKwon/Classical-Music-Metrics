Notes outside five octaves

At a glance

What the data show
Among the collections, Bach's English and French Suites, Beethoven's piano sonatas and Mozart's piano sonatas have the highest median (0.000) and Bach's English and French Suites, Beethoven's piano sonatas and Mozart's piano sonatas the lowest (0.000). Within the piano sonatas: Beethoven 0.000, Mozart 0.000.
Watch for
F1–F6 is a fixed reference for comparison.

Share of notes outside the five octaves F1–F6. Keyboard collections only (new in 1.9).

Formula
(notes below MIDI 29 or above MIDI 89) / all notes Grace notes are left out, and a tied continuation is merged into the note it continues and counted once. A continuation whose preceding note cannot be found is not counted as a new note (it remains a sounding note only for the top-line metrics).
How to read it
In the Classical-era keyboard collections (Mozart, Scarlatti, C. P. E. Bach and others) every movement is 0 (Koželuh: 1 movement of 49 above 0; Beethoven's sonatas: 29 of 91). In the Romantic piano collections, pieces above 0 range from 3 of 13 in Schumann's Kinderszenen to all 19 of Medtner's Tales.
Caveats
F1–F6 is a fixed reference for comparison. Real keyboard compasses differed by period and instrument, and a value of 0 does not mean the piece was written for a five-octave instrument. Not computed for string or vocal collections.
