Soft pedal use

At a glance

What the data show
Performances using the soft pedal: Bach 41.8% (41/98), Beethoven 92.3% (228/247), Mozart 85.7% (12/14).
Watch for
Same limits as the damper share (e-Competition recordings, the 64 threshold, pieces cut from longer recordings).

Share of performances that use the soft (left, una corda) pedal at least once (new in 1.4).

Formula
recordings with CC67 ≥ 64 at any moment / recordings (pieces cut from one recording count once). 95% Wilson score interval. For performances that use it, the share of time it is down is also given.
How to read it
Bach 41.8% (41 of 98; 95% interval 32.6–51.7%), Beethoven 92.3% (228 of 247; 88.3–95.0%), Mozart 85.7% (12 of 14; 60.1–96.0%). In recordings that use it, the median share of time down is 0.11 (Bach), 0.39 (Beethoven) and 0.34 (Mozart).
Caveats
Same limits as the damper share (e-Competition recordings, the 64 threshold, pieces cut from longer recordings). 'At least once' counts a brief touch as use. Mozart has only 6 pieces and 14 recordings.

Source

ASAP dataset (Foscarin et al., ISMIR 2020). Piano MIDI performances aligned beat-by-beat to scores: 169 Bach (Well-Tempered Clavier) and 271 Beethoven (sonatas); 169 and 259 of these have beat counts matching the score and only those are used. Version 1.1 adds Mozart: 6 movements (K.310/i, K.331/iii, K.332 complete, Fantasia K.475), 16 performances — one to five per piece, so they appear in tables but not in comparison charts. Performances come from MAESTRO (International Piano-e-Competition). https://github.com/fosfrancesco/asap-dataset · License: CC BY-NC-SA 4.0
