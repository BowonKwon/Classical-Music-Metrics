Harmonic rhythm

Chord labels per measure, from the annotators' Roman-numeral analysis.

Formula
chord labels / measures, where measures is the corpus metadata last_mn (last measure number without unfolding repeats)
How to read it
1 means one chord a bar; 3 means a change every beat in triple metre. Bach suites median 3.05, Beethoven sonatas and quartets 1.8.
Caveats
Only for annotated corpora (not chorales). Annotation granularity differs slightly between corpora.
