Harmonic rhythm

At a glance

What the data show
Among the collections, Bach's English and French Suites have the highest median (3.05) and Bach's solo works (violin, cello, flute) the lowest (1.77). Within the piano sonatas: Beethoven 1.83, Mozart 2.05.
Watch for
Only for annotated corpora (not chorales). Annotation granularity differs slightly between corpora, and bar length and meter differ between collections.

Chord labels per bar, from the annotators' Roman-numeral analysis.

Formula
chord labels / bars, where bars is the corpus metadata last_mn (last bar number without unfolding repeats)
How to read it
1 means one chord a bar; 3 means a change every beat in triple meter. Bach suites median 3.05, Beethoven sonatas and quartets 1.8.
Caveats
Only for annotated corpora (not chorales). Annotation granularity differs slightly between corpora.
