At a glance

What the data show
Among the collections, Bach's English and French Suites have the highest median (20.8) and Beethoven's piano sonatas the lowest (12.1). Within the piano sonatas: Beethoven 12.1, Mozart 16.5.
Watch for
Only where cadences are annotated: the Beethoven quartets have none, and among Bach's solo works only BWV 1009–1013 are annotated (the violin works and Cello Suites 1–2 are left blank). Treat differences between collections with different bar lengths with caution.

Annotated cadences per 100 bars.

Formula
100 × cadence labels / bars. Every cadence label counts, including those on rows without a chord symbol. Bars = the movement's last bar number.
How to read it
How often phrases close. Bach suites 20.8, Mozart sonatas 16.5, Bach solo works (BWV 1009–1013) 15.8, Beethoven sonatas 12.1.
Caveats
Only where cadences are annotated: the Beethoven quartets have none, and among Bach's solo works only BWV 1009–1013 are annotated (the violin works and Cello Suites 1–2 are left blank). Treat differences between collections with different bar lengths with caution.
