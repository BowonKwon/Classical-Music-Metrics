Mean simultaneity

At a glance

What the data show
Among the collections, Bach's chorales have the highest median (2.85) and Bach's solo works (violin, cello, flute) the lowest (1.03). Within the piano sonatas: Beethoven 1.81, Mozart 1.59.
Watch for
Counts starting notes, not sounding ones. All voices are counted together, so differences between collections with different forces largely reflect the forces.

Average number of notes starting together at each onset position.

Formula
note onsets / distinct onset positions Grace notes are left out, and a tied continuation is merged into the note it continues and counted once. A continuation whose preceding note cannot be found is not counted as a new note (it remains a sounding note only for the top-line metrics).
How to read it
Near 1 is a single line; chorales approach 3 (four voices move together, sometimes one alone).
Caveats
Counts starting notes, not sounding ones; held notes drop out.
