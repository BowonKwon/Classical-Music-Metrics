Beethoven share

At a glance

What the data show
1842–43 19.4% → 2025–26 2.0% (completed seasons). The peak was 1881–82 (23.7%; 13 concerts, 76 work performances).
Watch for
One archive's record, which mixes in programs of related bodies (New York Symphony, Stadium concerts). Early seasons have few work performances, so shares are coarse (31 in 1842–43).

Share of a season's work performances that are Beethoven.

Formula
Beethoven work performances / all work performances. One work performance = one work on a program × that program's number of concerts (entries the archive files under the same work number count once).
How to read it
From 19.4% in 1842–43 to 2.0% in 2025–26 (3.3% in 2022–23). Subscription concerts only: 25.0% to 3.1%.
Caveats
The counting unit changed in 1.1, so values differ from 1.0 (17.6% in 1842–43, 1.8% in 2025–26); the '1.0 unit' column keeps the old values.
