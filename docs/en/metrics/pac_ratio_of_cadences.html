Perfect authentic cadence share

At a glance

What the data show
Among the collections, Bach's solo works (violin, cello, flute) have the highest median (0.667) and Beethoven's piano sonatas the lowest (0.471). Within the piano sonatas: Beethoven 0.471, Mozart 0.491.
Watch for
Only for movements with cadence annotation (same scope as cadence density). Movements with few cadences give coarse values.

Share of cadences that are perfect authentic (PAC: V–I with the top voice ending on the tonic).

Formula
PAC labels / all cadence labels (including those on rows without a chord symbol). PAC follows the annotators' labels.
How to read it
The rest are imperfect authentic, half, plagal, deceptive and so on. Bach solo works 0.667 and suites 0.571, Mozart 0.491, Beethoven sonatas 0.471.
Caveats
Only for movements with cadence annotation (same scope as cadence density). Movements with few cadences give coarse values.
