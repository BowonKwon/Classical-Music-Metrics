Mean chord length

At a glance

What the data show
Among the collections, Bach's solo works (violin, cello, flute) have the highest median (1.81) and Bach's English and French Suites the lowest (1.16). Within the piano sonatas: Beethoven 1.64, Mozart 1.41.
Watch for
Only for annotated corpora (not chorales). Annotation granularity differs slightly between corpora.

Average length of one annotated chord (quarter note = 1).

Formula
mean(duration of chord labels)
How to read it
The beat-based version of harmonic rhythm, so collections in different meters compare more easily. Bach suites 1.16 (fast changes), solo works 1.81, Beethoven sonatas 1.64, Mozart 1.41.
Caveats
Only for annotated corpora (not chorales). Annotation granularity differs slightly between corpora. Beethoven's piano sonatas enter with the 64 of 91 movements that have harmony annotation.
