Performance metricsUnit: bpm

Tempo minus metronome mark

Tempo measured near the end of the track (the coda) minus Beethoven's metronome mark for that section.

Formula
measured tempo − mark. Where the measurement fell below 0.6× or above 1.4× the mark it was treated as a tempo-tracker harmonic and corrected by the nearest factor among 1/3, 1/2, 2/3, 3/2, 2, 3 (17 of 1,332 rows, all in Symphony 9, which the summaries exclude; none of the 1,188 Symphony 1–8 rows is corrected).
How to read it
−12 means twelve beats a minute slower. Median −12.7 for Romantic conductors, −8 under HIP influence, −6 for period-instrument conductors. Every conductor's 33-movement median is negative, yet 152 of the 1,188 single movements (12.8%) run faster than the mark.
Caveats
Tempi are the original authors' Marsyas estimates from a single coda sample. Symphony 9 is excluded from summaries, as in the source paper.
