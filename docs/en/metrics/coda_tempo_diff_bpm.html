Tempo minus metronome mark

At a glance

What the data show
In Symphonies 1–8, all 36 conductors have a median slower than the metronome mark. The median of conductor medians is −11.3 bpm; the conductor closest to the marks is still −1.0 bpm.
Watch for
Tempi are the original authors' Marsyas estimates from a single coda sample. Symphony 9 is excluded (Symphonies 1–8 only). All conductor medians being negative does not mean every movement is slower.

Tempo measured near the end of the track (the coda) minus Beethoven's metronome mark for that section.

Formula
measured tempo − mark. Where the measurement fell below 0.6× or above 1.4× the mark it was treated as a tempo-tracker harmonic and corrected by the nearest factor among 1/3, 1/2, 2/3, 3/2, 2, 3 (17 of 1,332 rows, all in Symphony 9, which the summaries exclude; none of the 1,188 Symphony 1–8 rows is corrected).
How to read it
−12 means twelve beats a minute slower. Median −12.7 for Romantic conductors, −8 for HIP-influenced conductors, −6 for period-instrument conductors. Every conductor's 33-movement median is negative, yet 152 of the 1,188 single movements (12.8%) run faster than the mark.
Caveats
Tempi are the original authors' Marsyas estimates from a single coda sample. Symphony 9 is excluded from summaries, as in the source paper.
