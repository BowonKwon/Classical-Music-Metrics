Seventh-chord ratio

At a glance

What the data show
Among the collections, Beethoven's string quartets have the highest median (0.309) and Bach's solo works (violin, cello, flute) the lowest (0.211). Within the piano sonatas: Beethoven 0.309, Mozart 0.264.
Watch for
Ninths and added tones are not counted separately.

Share of chords containing a seventh.

Formula
labels with figured bass 7, 65, 43 or 2 (also 42) / chord labels
How to read it
Beethoven sonatas and quartets sit near 0.3, above Bach suites (0.24).
Caveats
Ninths and added tones are not counted separately. The denominator of chord labels includes the rare no-chord label (@none).
