Minor-key share

At a glance

What the data show
Among the collections, Bach's English and French Suites have the highest median (0.710) and Mozart's piano sonatas the lowest (0.096). Within the piano sonatas: Beethoven 0.112, Mozart 0.096.
Watch for
Driven by the movement's home key; to see a lean toward minor, compare major-key movements with each other.

Share of chord labels whose local key is minor.

Formula
chord labels with a minor localkey / all chord labels
How to read it
Near 1 for a minor-key movement; within a major-key movement it rises with the number of chord labels in minor passages (a count of labels, not a share of time). Bach suites median 0.71 and solo works 0.62 (many minor movements), Beethoven sonatas 0.11, Mozart 0.10.
Caveats
Driven by the movement's home key; to see a lean toward minor, compare major-key movements with each other. Only for annotated corpora. Beethoven's piano sonatas enter with the 64 of 91 movements that have harmony annotation.
