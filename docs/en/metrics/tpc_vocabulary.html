Pitch-name vocabulary (line of fifths)

At a glance

What the data show
Among the collections, Beethoven's string quartets have the highest median (17) and Bach's chorales the lowest (11). Within the piano sonatas: Beethoven 16, Mozart 15.
Watch for
Tends to grow with movement length.

Number of distinct pitch names used in the movement, counting C♯ and D♭ separately.

Formula
number of distinct tpc (line-of-fifths pitch names) in the movement Grace notes are left out, and a tied continuation is merged into the note it continues and counted once. A continuation whose preceding note cannot be found is not counted as a new note (it remains a sounding note only for the top-line metrics).
How to read it
Seven diatonic notes give 7; all twelve in one spelling give 12; more than 12 means some pitches appear under more than one spelling. Bach chorales 11, Beethoven quartets 17 and piano sonatas 16, Mozart 15. Unlike the signature-based chromatic ratio it separates enharmonic spellings; it tends to grow with motion to distant keys, but borrowed notes, notational habit and length also raise it.
Caveats
Tends to grow with movement length. Depends on notational practice (which name a pitch is given).
