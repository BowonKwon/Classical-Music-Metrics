Chromatic ratio

Share of notes outside the key signature in force in that measure (notes needing an accidental).

Formula
notes outside the seven diatonic pitch classes of the key signature / all notes. Diatonic membership uses tonal pitch class (line of fifths): with k sharps, tpc k−1 … k+5 is diatonic.
How to read it
0.06 means 6 of every 100 notes lie outside the key signature. Movements that modulate often or move chromatically score higher.
Caveats
Keyed to the signature, so a long modulation without a signature change counts entirely as chromatic. The DCML chorale encoding warns of accidental errors; it was cross-checked against the Humdrum encoding (r = 0.98).
