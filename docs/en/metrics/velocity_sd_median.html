Dynamic spread

At a glance

What the data show
Median over pieces: Bach 11.3 (59 pieces), Beethoven 16.6 (56 pieces). Mozart (6 pieces) is in the table only.
Watch for
MIDI velocity is a proxy for loudness and its scale depends on instrument and setup. Use it only for relative comparison within this dataset.

Standard deviation of key-strike velocity (MIDI velocity, 0–127) within a performance.

Formula
per performance sd(velocity) (population SD); piece value is the median across performances
How to read it
About 11 for Bach's Well-Tempered Clavier, about 17 for Beethoven sonatas — wider dynamic contrast in Beethoven.
Caveats

Source

ASAP dataset (Foscarin et al., ISMIR 2020). Piano MIDI performances aligned beat-by-beat to scores: 169 Bach (Well-Tempered Clavier) and 271 Beethoven (sonatas); 169 and 259 of these have beat counts matching the score and only those are used. Version 1.1 adds Mozart: 6 movements (K.310/i, K.331/iii, K.332 complete, Fantasia K.475), 16 performances — one to five per piece, so they appear in tables but not in comparison charts. Performances come from MAESTRO (International Piano-e-Competition). https://github.com/fosfrancesco/asap-dataset · License: CC BY-NC-SA 4.0
