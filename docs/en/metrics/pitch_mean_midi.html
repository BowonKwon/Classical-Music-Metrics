Mean pitch

At a glance

What the data show
Among the collections, Mozart's piano sonatas have the highest median (66.7) and Bach's solo works (violin, cello, flute) the lowest (55.1). Within the piano sonatas: Beethoven 62.5, Mozart 66.7.
Watch for
Driven by forces and instrument ranges; compare within the same forces only. Bach's solo works mix violin (high) and cello (low), so their distribution splits in two.

Average pitch of all notes in the movement (MIDI number; 60 = middle C).

Formula
mean(MIDI), weighted by note count Grace notes are left out, and a tied continuation is merged into the note it continues and counted once. A continuation whose preceding note cannot be found is not counted as a new note (it remains a sounding note only for the top-line metrics).
How to read it
Mozart's piano sonatas (median 66.7, between a fourth and a fifth above middle C) sit higher than Beethoven's (62.5). Bach's solo works mix violin (high) and cello (low): median 55.1 but a split distribution.
Caveats
Driven by forces and instrument ranges; compare within the same forces only.
