Top-line leap ratio

Share of moves in the highest line that span a minor third or more.

Formula
top line = highest sounding note at each onset position, taken only where that note itself starts there. moves ≥ 3 semitones / all moves
How to read it
Chorale sopranos are nearly all stepwise (about 0.15); arpeggiated solo works exceed 0.4.
Caveats
The 'top line' is the chain of highest notes, not a designated voice, so it can differ from the true melody where voices cross.
