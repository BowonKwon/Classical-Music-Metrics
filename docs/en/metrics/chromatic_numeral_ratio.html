Chromatic chord ratio

At a glance

What the data show
Among the collections, Bach's solo works (violin, cello, flute) have the highest median (0.068) and Mozart's piano sonatas the lowest (0.026). Within the piano sonatas: Beethoven 0.041, Mozart 0.026.
Watch for
DCML writes minor-key numerals against the natural minor scale, so minor-key leading-tone chords (diatonic in harmonic minor) appear as ♯vii and count here (206 of 2,304 minor-key chords in Mozart). Groups with more minor music score higher.

Share of chords whose Roman numeral carries ♯ or ♭ (♭VI, ♭II, ♯vii …), mostly chords whose root lies outside the key's natural scale.

Formula
chord labels whose Roman numeral carries ♯ or ♭ / all chord labels
How to read it
A different axis of chromaticism from applied chords (an applied chord can have a diatonic root). Bach solo works 0.068 and suites 0.051, Beethoven sonatas 0.041, Mozart 0.026.
Caveats
DCML writes minor-key numerals against the natural minor scale, so minor-key leading-tone chords (diatonic in harmonic minor) appear as ♯vii and count here (206 of 2,304 minor-key chords in Mozart). Groups with more minor music score higher. Only for annotated corpora. Beethoven's piano sonatas enter with the 64 of 91 movements that have harmony annotation.
