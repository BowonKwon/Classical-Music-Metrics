Pedal-point ratio

At a glance

What the data show
Among the collections, Beethoven's string quartets have the highest median (0.083) and Bach's English and French Suites and Bach's solo works (violin, cello, flute) the lowest (0.000). Within the piano sonatas: Beethoven 0.068, Mozart 0.064.
Watch for
Counts only passages the annotators marked as pedal.

Share of chords that sit over a sustained bass (pedal point).

Formula
chord labels with the pedal field filled / all chord labels
How to read it
Beethoven quartets 0.083, sonatas 0.068, Mozart 0.064. Bach suites and solo works have a median of 0 (few movements use one).
Caveats
Counts only passages the annotators marked as pedal. Only for annotated corpora (not chorales). Beethoven's piano sonatas enter with the 64 of 91 movements that have harmony annotation.
