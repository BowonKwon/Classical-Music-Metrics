Columns: Beethoven symphony recordings

In beethoven_symphony_recordings.csv (and the coda_tempo and duration_ratio tables that carry it), date is the recording period, as written in the source (bmetr): a single year ('1995') or a range ('1977-1979', '1972- 1975'). year is not the recording year but the release year of the album set (bmetr documentation: release date). The source's year column contains the values 2911 and 2912, which cannot be release years, unchanged. Wherever this site needs the time of recording it uses date: recording_year_start is the first year in date, and rec_year_first, rec_year_last and rec_year_mid in the tempo-trend table (beethoven_symphony_conductor_year.csv) are its first, last and middle years.
