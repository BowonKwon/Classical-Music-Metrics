What sabermetrics changed in baseball was not the statistics themselves but publishing each metric's definition, formula, sample and caveats. This site applies that frame to Western classical music: numbers extracted from open score corpora and performance datasets, one page per metric. It began with Bach and Beethoven; version 1.1 added Mozart and the New York Philharmonic's repertoire covering every composer.
