What sabermetrics changed in baseball was not the statistics themselves but publishing each metric's definition, formula, sample and caveats. This site applies that frame to Western classical music: numbers extracted from open score corpora and performance datasets, one page per metric. The first edition covers Bach and Beethoven.

Score metrics

Pitch rangeDistance between the highest and lowest note in the movement.Chromatic ratioShare of notes outside the key signature in force in that measure (notes needing an accidental).Pitch-class entropyHow evenly the twelve pitch classes are used.Rhythm entropyHow evenly different note durations are mixed.Off-beat onset ratioShare of notes that do not start on a beat of the time signature's unit (the quarter in 3/4).Note densityNumber of note onsets per quarter-note beat, all parts combined.Mean simultaneityAverage number of notes starting together at each onset position.Top-line leap ratioShare of moves in the highest line that span a minor third or more.Top-line mean intervalAverage distance in semitones between neighbouring notes of the highest line.Harmonic rhythmChord labels per measure, from the annotators' Roman-numeral analysis.Chord vocabularyDistinct chord labels per 100 labels.Applied-chord ratioShare of chords applied to another key's tonic (secondary dominants such as V/V).Seventh-chord ratioShare of chords containing a seventh.Modulation rateNumber of changes of the annotated local key, per 100 measures.

Performance metrics

Tempo minus metronome markTempo measured near the end of the track (the coda) minus Beethoven's metronome mark for that section.Performed / metronome durationTrack length divided by the theoretical length if played at Beethoven's marks without repeats.Across-performer tempo spreadHow much performers of the same piece differ in average tempo.Beat-to-beat tempo changeMedian deviation of the ratio of neighbouring beat intervals from 1 — the size of rubato.Within-performance tempo variationDispersion of beat intervals within one performance (coefficient of variation), including sectional contrasts such as slow introduction vs fast main section, not only rubato.Dynamic spreadStandard deviation of key-strike velocity (MIDI velocity, 0–127) within a performance.
